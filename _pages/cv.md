## Project Experience

**Industrial Image Classification — MAGYAR SA** · Mulhouse, France · June 2025
Delivered an end-to-end offline CNN prototype for industrial tank categorization. Built and curated a proprietary dataset of ~1,000 images from scratch. Achieved 78% F1-score under real constraints: class imbalance, visual ambiguity, and uncontrolled acquisition conditions. Translated business requirements into evaluation criteria with the engineering team.

**CNN Stock Prediction — Reproducibility Study** · Chicoutimi, QC · January 2026
Reimplemented a published CNN pipeline for stock movement prediction and evaluated it under a strict leakage-free temporal protocol. Demonstrated that no model generalizes beyond a naive always-BUY baseline (62.7% accuracy), attributing the original paper's 91% claim to look-ahead bias. Shared publicly; reached 65,000+ views.

**Large-Scale Sentiment Analysis on 1.6M Tweets** · Chicoutimi, QC · Nov–Dec 2025
Designed a distributed sentiment classification pipeline using PySpark on a Calcul Québec HPC cluster. Applied Approximate kNN with MinHash LSH across four feature engineering strategies. Achieved 85.4% accuracy (binary, unigrammes) and confirmed near-linear scalability with dataset size. Also evaluated Bloom Filter-based vectorization, documenting its limitations.

**Diabetes Risk Prediction — EDA-Driven ML** · Kaggle Playground S5E12
Approached a 700K-sample binary classification task with EDA-first methodology: estimated a ROC-AUC ceiling of ~0.72–0.73 from feature overlap analysis, then confirmed it with LightGBM (0.727 OOF). Conducted SHAP interpretation, clinical error profiling, calibration analysis, and covariate shift detection. Deliberately avoided leaderboard overfitting.
